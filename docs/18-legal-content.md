# Legal & Content Handling

## Principles

The product aggregates and geolocates public news metadata; it is not the publisher of the underlying articles.

Prefer retaining:

- source;
- title;
- URL;
- timestamps;
- limited excerpt/summary necessary for product functionality.

Always attribute the original source.

## Source policy

For each source maintain:

- allowed ingestion method;
- terms/limitations;
- robots policy where applicable;
- disable switch.

Do not bypass anti-bot/access controls.

## Copyright-conscious summaries

AI summaries should be original summaries based on source data, not a substitute for the full article.

Users should have direct access to the original article link.

## Future TTS

TTS should read the product's original event summary/brief, not automatically reproduce entire copyrighted articles.
